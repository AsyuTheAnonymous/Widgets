What's the difference between the Basic and Advanced website plans?

Basic sites are built on Hostinger's drag-and-drop builder. They load fast, work on every phone, and suit small businesses and portfolios.

Advanced sites (+$300) run on WordPress, which opens up online stores, member logins, and thousands of plugins for businesses with bigger needs.

Do I need a website to get business email?

No. Custom email only needs a domain. We register the domain (or use one you already own), set up your mailboxes, and connect them to your phone and computer. You can add a website later.

How is website pricing calculated?

Your quote is your plan, plus $100 for each page, plus any features you add, such as contact forms, online booking, or a photo gallery. The inquiry form shows the total as you pick options, so there are no surprises.

What does managed VPS hosting include?

We handle the technical setup so you don't have to. You get a server that's configured and ready, plus the login details to start using it right away.

Can you move my existing website?

Yes. We migrate existing WordPress sites, including content, media, and settings. Your current site stays live until the new one is ready to switch over.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

You'll get a proposal with scope, price, and timeline. Once you approve it, we build a live preview you can check on your own phone, make revisions with you, and launch when you're happy. Start with a quick message.
